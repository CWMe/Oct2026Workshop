Technical learning should be accessible, collaborative, and free.

Code With Me was founded in 2017 by software professional Shijit Dasgupta to give people in the region a place to explore new technologies and make real connections.

We bring together developers, software engineers, DevOps professionals, cloud engineers, and curious learners—from first-time coders to seasoned architects. We emphasize pair programming and a culture of kindness, and we've worked with more than a dozen companies to learn what skills are in demand.
