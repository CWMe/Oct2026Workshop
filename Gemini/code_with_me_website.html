Our Mission & Community

Empowering the NOVA & DC Developer Ecosystem

Code With Me was founded in 2017 on a simple premise: technical learning is faster, more engaging, and more impactful when we do it together.

Hands-On Live Coding

Bring your laptop. We build full-stack apps, write scripts, and solve real-world programming problems in real-time.

Cloud & DevOps Labs

Explore AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, Docker, and CI/CD pipelines with industry practitioners and certified engineers.

Technical Talks

Deep dives into modern tech stacks, AI/ML tools, system architecture, and modern web frameworks hosted by expert speakers.

Free & Inclusive

Whether you are a junior developer or a principal architect, our meetups are always free, friendly, and collaborative.
