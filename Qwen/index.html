About Us

More than a meetup — a movement to level up together.

Who we are

Founded in 2017, Code With Me brings developers, software engineers, DevOps professionals, cloud engineers, and technology enthusiasts together to learn, build, and grow. Our meetup is centered on the exchange of ideas in software engineering — whether you're looking to learn or teach, you'll find a supportive environment here.

🎯

Our mission

To make technical learning accessible, collaborative, and free for everyone in the NoVA & DC tech scene.

🛠️

What we host

Technical talks, hands-on workshops, live coding sessions, debugging exercises, and networking events — often with companies like Amazon Web Services.

💛

Our culture

We believe in the power of pair programming and fostering a culture of kindness — everyone feels welcome and is supported.

🚀

Why members love it

Explore new languages, techniques, and toolkits — and see what's "hot" in the market right now, connecting you with local companies.

What We Do

Ways to show up and level up.

Every event is hands-on, in-person, and free. Pick your flavor.

Technical Talks

Hear from engineers and industry leaders — from cutting-edge frameworks to career insights and market trends.

Hands-On Workshops

Dig into real toolkits and techniques with guided, practical sessions — build something while you learn.

Live Coding

Code together in real time. Watch, question, and participate as problems are solved on the spot.

Debugging Exercises

Sharpen your problem-solving skills in collaborative debugging drills — a great way to learn by doing.

Networking Events

Meet your next teammate, mentor, or hiring manager. Connecting with local companies is part of what we're about.

Pair Programming

A core value of our community. Learn faster, write better code, and support each other — one pair at a time.
