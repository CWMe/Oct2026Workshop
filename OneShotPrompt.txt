About Code With Me
Founded in 2017, Code With Me brings developers, software engineers, DevOps professionals, cloud engineers, and technology enthusiasts together to learn, build, and grow.
We primarily organize in-person technology meetups across Northern Virginia and Washington, DC, collaborating with companies such as Amazon Web Services and other technology organizations to host technical talks, workshops, live coding sessions, debugging exercises, and networking events.
Our mission is to make technical learning accessible, collaborative, and free.
